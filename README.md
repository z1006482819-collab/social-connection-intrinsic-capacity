# Social Connection and Intrinsic Capacity in Adults Aged 50 Years and Older

## Overview

This project explores the association between **social connection** and **intrinsic capacity (IC)** among adults aged 50 years and older. It is a reproducible evidence-synthesis and data-visualisation project developed alongside an MPH capstone systematic review.

Intrinsic capacity is a key concept in the World Health Organization's healthy ageing framework and refers to the combination of physical and mental capacities that an individual can draw upon. Social connection is multidimensional; this project therefore distinguishes between loneliness, social isolation, social participation or engagement, and social network characteristics rather than treating them as interchangeable.

## Aim

To systematically examine the association between social connection and intrinsic capacity among adults aged 50 years and older.

## Objectives

1. Identify and synthesise quantitative evidence examining social connection as an exposure in relation to intrinsic capacity among adults aged 50 years and older.
2. Examine whether associations with intrinsic capacity differ across dimensions of social connection—including loneliness, social isolation, social participation or engagement, and social network characteristics—and across overall IC and individual IC domains where reported.
3. Where sufficient evidence is available, explore whether associations differ across population characteristics such as age, sex and geographical setting.

## Conceptual Framework

### Social connection dimensions
- Loneliness
- Social isolation
- Social participation / engagement
- Social network characteristics

### Intrinsic capacity outcomes
- Overall intrinsic capacity
- Locomotion
- Cognition
- Psychological capacity
- Vitality
- Sensory capacity

## Planned Workflow

1. Literature search and study screening
2. Data extraction
3. Study and variable classification
4. Descriptive evidence synthesis
5. Reproducible analysis in Python or R
6. Evidence visualisation
7. Interpretation of findings and research gaps

Potential visualisations include an evidence map across social-connection constructs and IC outcomes, geographical distribution of included studies, study-design summaries, and—where effect estimates are sufficiently comparable—forest plots.

## Current Status

**Work in progress.** The systematic review and evidence extraction are ongoing. Results and conclusions will be added only after the relevant analyses are completed.

## Research Note

Observed associations should not be interpreted as evidence of causation. The direction of the relationship may be bidirectional: social connection may be associated with maintenance of intrinsic capacity, while reduced intrinsic capacity may also limit opportunities for social participation.

## Data and Academic Integrity

This repository is intended as a public research portfolio and reproducibility project. Assessment materials, copyrighted full-text articles, confidential data, and non-public research data will not be uploaded. Only materials appropriate for public sharing will be included.
